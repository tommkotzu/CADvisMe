My workflow.

One continuous path from engineering data to the finished image – traceable and repeatable.

  1. 01

    Import

    STEP, IGES, CATIA, JT or KiCad, straight from engineering.

  2. 02

    Preparation

    Clean geometry, structured assemblies, materials assigned.

  3. 03

    Look development

    Materials, light and camera defined, style agreed.

  4. 04

    Rendering & animation

    Stills, exploded views and camera moves.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Formats for web, print, trade fairs and presentations.
